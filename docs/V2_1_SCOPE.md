# v2.1 scope and rationale

The v2OAI baseline has strong outcome/evidence/authority/recovery rules, 25 methods, a complete source compiler and defensive archive tools. It starts from an item request and its success criterion is an accepted artifact. A new founder needs a clear bridge from those mechanisms to a company that can actually deliver and continue.

The main gap is not another department, platform or mandatory tracker. It is an understandable entry, evidence that separates a plan from operation, cash and delivery constraints, and a small next action. The old 00 template also prefilled a 72-hour pilot, a Drive tree and Day-1 record expectations despite the lean operating contract. This edition makes those conditional and preserves the record fields.

## Decision

Add a company profile with Frame, Test, Deliver and Operate gates. These describe observed business readiness; the existing Ingestion/Firming/Mandate/Blitzing/Delivering stages remain optional workflow mechanics. A gate records Proceed, Hold or Walk, evidence, owner and permitted next action. No prescribed interview count, revenue target or 72-hour company promise applies.

Reuse one company brief and a cash table when existing artifacts do not already cover the required information. The brief connects customer/problem/payer, offer and price, route to customers, delivery requirements, responsibilities, evidence, next experiment and cash assumptions. Do not generate a strategy, GTM, BRD, governance pack or recurring ritual just to fill a template.

Provide standard-library local tools for initialization, cash arithmetic and shared-CSV scoping. Keep evidence assessment and approvals with the responsible human. A successful command says the command met its contract, not that customers want the offer or that the activity is compliant.

## Preserved and changed

Preserve all 25 method identities, required resources/notices, 00/03/09/10 names, one canonical writer, adoption through explicit decisions, scoped evidence, revision-bound changes, reconcile-before-retry, archive defenses and runtime/builder separation.

Replace personal writing samples and private-case-derived fixtures with configurable guidance and independently synthetic material. Remove account IDs, personal environment instructions and live installation/pilot records. Keep originals outside repository history. Explicit licensing notices remain with imported components.

Historical export failures justify two specific cases: a permitted shared CSV can still contain unrelated rows/columns, and an editable receiving copy can retain obsolete parent startup instructions after its calculations change. Content scoping/provenance and truthful active identity must be checked separately from archive integrity.

## Out of scope

No SaaS platform, paid dependency, native skill installation, scheduler, company incorporation, actual customer outreach, payments, filings or automatic compliance claim. No migration overwrites existing company records. Public visibility and a repository-wide redistribution license remain separate decisions.

## Acceptance

The release is reviewable through source, exact payload hashes, synthetic examples, a fresh-reader walkthrough, adversarial/regression tests, repeatable packaging, content/history review, independent review of the saved commit, and observed private remote state/CI. Unrun and platform-blocked checks stay distinct from passes.
