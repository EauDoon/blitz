# Blitz v2.1OAI company profile

Use this profile when the user commissions a minimum viable company (MVC), a company operating pack, or work on an existing company's operating loop. It builds on the [operating contract](../setup/operating-contract.md), with the same evidence, authority, privacy, one-writer, recovery and export rules. A request for a memo, review, prototype or plan keeps its narrower scope. Selecting this profile authorizes no external action by itself.

## What counts as a minimum viable company

An MVC is a bounded business that has demonstrated one complete value-delivery cycle for an identified customer and payer, with an accountable human owner able to run the next cycle. Its evidence covers a clear offer, an accepted delivery, the agreed exchange, support and failure handling, delivery capacity, cash obligations and the permissions relevant to that activity and market. For a commercial offer, the exchange includes verified payment; a signed contract, invoice or expression of interest alone does not establish collection.

An operating plan can be ready for an authorized test before an MVC is demonstrated. Use these distinct claims:

| Claim | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Prepared for the next experiment | Bounded offer, named owner, evidence-based experiment, time/cash cap, acceptance and stop criteria, and authority for the next action. Material dependencies are named. |
| MVC demonstrated for this offer and scope | At least one observed customer cycle reaches agreed acceptance and exchange; actual costs/cash are reconciled; a feasible next cycle has an owner, capacity and applicable permissions. State date, scope and limits. |
| Repetition demonstrated | Further observed cycles support the specific repeatability claim under the relevant conditions. One successful cycle is insufficient. |

These are operating claims. Incorporation, a polished pack, a working prototype and passing package tests do not establish them. MVC status does not imply profitability, product-market fit, scalable demand, legal clearance or investment readiness. A business with long delivery or payment cycles remains at its observed state; do not redefine the cycle to manufacture completion. A deliberately free experiment may validate use or delivery, but it does not establish paid demand. Noncommercial programs use their commissioned outcome and funding evidence under the existing program route.

## Start with the smallest useful record

Recover existing evidence and decisions first. Establish the customer and payer, promised result, narrow offer, initial route to customers, minimum delivery requirements, owner, governing constraints, hardest unproven assumption and next permitted action. A sales route names the channel, concrete ask, owner, test metric and applicable outreach authority. Minimum requirements say what must work for the promised outcome and how failure is detected; a full product specification is optional. Ask only about consequential gaps that cannot be resolved from authorized sources. Use a [company brief](../templates/company-brief.md) if these are scattered; existing sufficient records need no duplicate brief.

Use 00 for locations, selected profile, owners and current work; 03 for the adopted position and decision rights; 09 for material unknowns and the evidence that would settle them; 10 for actual decisions, gate results and revisions. The brief links to those records where they already hold the answer. File 04 or a sufficient existing model holds economics. File 05 is created only when a material jurisdiction or activity question needs a durable map. No deck, corporate department, CRM, background routine or full context tree is mandatory.

Do not present a template as a company. In a reusable kit, blank inputs are appropriate. In commissioned company work, retrieve available inputs, populate required content and identify the exact remaining blockers.

## Evidence gates for the next commitment

Company readiness is an evidence axis separate from the playbook's Ingestion, Firming, Mandate, Blitzing and Delivering work stages. It is not a 72-hour company promise. Revisit only gates affected by new evidence, scope or conditions. A company can deliver a safe service while a proposed regulated feature remains on Hold.

Before each material experiment or commitment, define observable pass, change and stop criteria, the evidence source, owner, deadline or observation horizon, and maximum cash/time exposure. Choose thresholds for the cost of being wrong. There is no universal interview count, conversion target, revenue target or number of documents.

| Gate | Evidence that supports the next step | Typical Hold condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | Named customer, payer and problem; bounded offer and price or test price; measurable outcome and exclusions; accountable owner; hardest assumption; permitted next test and cash/time cap. Distinguish observed problems from founder beliefs. | The next action lacks an owner, authority, a bounded risk or an intelligible customer outcome. |
| **Test** | Direct evidence from the intended customer and payer, with the behavior observed and its limits. An interview establishes what was said; a booking, contract and payment establish different things. Evaluate against the criteria fixed before the test. | Praise is being treated as demand; the buying decision or critical requirement is untested; exposure exceeds the authorized cap. |
| **Deliver** | Agreed scope and acceptance, actual delivery evidence, customer acceptance or the exact rejection, support/refund path, and a named owner for failure. Use real artifacts and receipts for live claims. | Delivery is unsafe, the promised result cannot be checked, required permissions are unresolved, or fulfillment/cancellation responsibilities are unclear. |
| **Operate** | Reconciled exchange and obligations; contribution and cash timing; realistic capacity and dependencies; a short next-cycle procedure with owner, checks, failure route and stop conditions. State what one cycle does and does not prove. | Collection is assumed, unpaid obligations are omitted, cash falls below the decision owner's floor, labor/capacity is missing, or repeating delivery depends on an unavailable person or permission. |

Record Proceed, Hold or Walk for the specific next commitment, with its evidence and permitted continuing work. Proceed requires the applicable evidence and existing authority; a gate does not grant approval. Hold pauses only the dependent commitment while safe preparation continues. Walk rejects that option for the stated scope, preserving useful evidence. A decision owner can revise a commercial preference or risk budget within their authority; an override cannot waive law, another person's rights, required permissions or host restrictions.

Failed tests should change the offer, method or decision. Keep the original criteria and observed result, then record any amended test separately. Never improve a result by rewriting its threshold afterward.

## Match rigor to consequences

| Next action | Proportionate evidence and control |
|---|---|
| Reversible internal exploration using permitted nonsensitive material | A bounded assumption and small time/cash budget may suffice. Label the output and avoid unsupported customer claims. |
| Customer delivery, spending, sensitive information or consequential claims | Verify load-bearing inputs, acceptance, commercial terms, data handling, recovery/refund obligations and the human authority for the exact commitment. Use independent review when a material defect would harm the customer or business. |
| Activity involving regulated services, customer funds, health/safety, material security exposure or difficult-to-reverse obligations | Identify activity, jurisdictions and parties. Obtain current primary-source evidence and qualified legal, tax, licensing, security or domain review where needed. Resolve material scope questions before the affected commitment. A mockup or sandbox test can proceed only within its own permitted scope. |

Industry labels do not decide the gate. The same company can have low-risk internal work and a high-consequence customer action. Neither a generic disclaimer nor a founder's assertion resolves a substantive constraint.

## People and operating authority

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Accountable founder or decision owner | Owns the offer, risk budget and business decisions; specifies delegation and reserved decisions; accepts or changes the position of record. |
| Operator or builder | Produces and checks the authorized work, preserves evidence, and escalates a material exception with a concrete recommendation. An AI assistant can fill this role within granted authority. |
| Reviewer or qualified adviser | Independently checks the consequential artifact or scoped domain question when required. A reviewed artifact is not an authorization to execute it. |
| Canonical scribe | Maintains the one current position and necessary receipts; reconciles revision-bound DELTAs and interrupted actions. |

One human may fill several roles in a small company. Add people only for capacity, expertise or genuine independence. AI does not become a legal officer, account holder, signer, employer or licensed professional through a role label.

Use explicit task authority for outreach, publication, formation/filing, contracts, payments, account or credential changes, hiring and production deployment. Existing specific authorization remains valid within its recorded scope and limits; do not ask again merely because a gate has advanced. A broad request to prepare a company is not authority to perform these actions. Prepare the concrete, reviewable result up to the boundary, then obtain only the missing decision. Retain actual action receipts and reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying.

## Cash, economics and capacity

Before money is committed, show a rough model covering the next material cash obligations and the decision horizon. Use [company-cash.csv](../templates/company-cash.csv), file 04 or an existing sufficient model. Choose explicit periods, currency, as-of date, units and sources. Separate actuals, estimates and assumptions. Unknown is not zero. A cash model with incomplete inputs is incomplete even when a subtotal can be computed.

At minimum, distinguish booked orders, earned revenue, invoices, gross collections and refunds; variable incurred delivery costs from cash payments; fixed and one-time costs; tax payment timing; financing from revenue; unrestricted opening cash from customer or restricted funds. Track committed but unpaid obligations in the appropriate future periods. Never count customer funds held for others as working capital.

Compute and inspect in code:

* Closing cash = opening cash + gross cash receipts + financing in - refunds paid - variable cash costs - fixed cash costs - one-time cash costs - financing out - tax paid.
* Contribution = earned revenue, net of revenue reductions, minus variable incurred delivery costs. State what those costs include. Contribution per unit and margin require valid nonzero denominators and consistent units/periods.
* Available cash above the chosen floor = closing cash minus the reserve floor. A negative value identifies a funding/timing problem for a decision; it does not authorize borrowing or spending. A reserve floor is a planning control, not a cash outflow.
* Capacity = actual or evidenced delivery hours required versus available delivery hours in the same period. Include founder work, support, rework and bottlenecks. Unpaid founder time is still a capacity cost; where decision-useful, show its imputed economic cost separately with a stated rate.

Avoid double counting a refund or paid founder compensation across categories. Revenue and costs may belong to a different period from their receipts/payments. A constant-burn runway ratio is only a rough diagnostic when its assumptions hold; dated cash obligations and collections govern the next commitment. Stress the decisive assumptions, such as slower collection, lower sales, higher delivery cost or founder unavailability. State the action that would follow a shortfall.

The optional `setup/company.py` helper creates local starter records and computes the declared CSV schedule. Follow its `--help` for supported commands and validation. It cannot infer missing costs, verify sources, resolve tax treatment, approve an action or certify company readiness. No helper is required to use this profile.

## Jurisdiction and activity checks, only where needed

For the proposed next activity, identify the relevant location of operation, customer, worker, entity and money/data flow. Consider formation/registration, tax and invoicing, contracts, employment/contractors, privacy/security, intellectual property, sector licenses and insurance only to the extent they can affect that activity. These are discovery prompts, not a complete legal checklist and not a claim that every company requires each item.

Reuse existing verified advice or obtain current primary sources and qualified advice where the consequence warrants it. Record the issue, scope, source/date, owner, unresolved question, next commitment affected, and the event that requires refresh. A finding of not applicable needs a scoped basis; unknown stays unknown. Neither an unregulated industry label nor incorporation proves compliance. Filing, signing, opening accounts and paying charges remain separately authorized actions. The kit performs none automatically.

## Acceptance and continuation

Deliver the commissioned artifact and an evidence-bound readiness statement: what has been demonstrated, for which offer/customer scope and date, the next permitted action, and any material blocker. Use the existing [acceptance record](../templates/acceptance-template.md) when a durable record is useful. Keep accepted decisions separate from factual verification.

For a company pack, another authorized operator should be able to identify the offer, current evidence, obligations, accountable owner, next cycle and stop conditions using the saved records. Test that handoff proportionately. Export only the requested allowlisted content under the existing [handoff contract](../templates/handoff.md); customer records, credentials and private material are not reusable kit content.

In the master kit, the [synthetic service example](../examples/company/service-example.md) shows the whole reasoning path. The [synthetic regulated hold](../examples/company/regulated-hold.md) shows why a prototype and money model may be complete while a live commitment remains blocked. Examples are training fixtures, deliberately excluded from runtime views, and are not required to execute this profile. They provide no real demand, transaction, legal or performance evidence.
