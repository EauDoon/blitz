# Blitz v2.1OAI-r1 company and department profile

Blitz is a **Minimum Viable Company kit**.

Listen, collect, sprint.<br>Reduce cycle time.

Autoscale and quickly launch a minimum viable company or department that can stand up in minimal time.

Use this profile to establish a minimum viable company (MVC), start an internal department, or improve an existing operating loop. Work directly with an editor and the records, or use an LLM with permitted sources and appropriate attached tools. The kit supplies methods, records and local helpers. The operator supplies the evidence, access and authority for the work.

Target a demonstrated company or department cycle and a usable operating unit in one or two days. That depends on the offer, evidence, authority, capacity, and time needed for delivery, acceptance and any payment. Record the actual state when these conditions prevent the target. Here, autoscale means make a proven process repeatable and expand only with evidence, capacity and authority. The kit does not deploy autonomous infrastructure.

This profile builds on the [operating contract](../setup/operating-contract.md), with the same evidence, authority, privacy, one-writer, recovery and export rules. A request for a memo, review, prototype or plan keeps its narrower scope. Selecting this profile authorizes no external action by itself.

## What counts as a minimum viable company

An MVC is a bounded business that has demonstrated one complete value-delivery cycle for an identified customer and payer. An accountable human owner must be able to run the next cycle. Evidence must cover a clear offer, accepted delivery, agreed exchange, support and failure handling, delivery capacity, cash obligations and applicable permissions. For a commercial offer, the exchange includes verified payment. A signed contract, invoice or expression of interest alone does not establish collection.

An internal department reaches its operating baseline through an observed delivery cycle for named internal users or stakeholders. The designated recipient and accountable sponsor must accept it against agreed criteria. The department also needs authorized resources, reconciled actual budget and capacity use, reconciled obligations, failure handling, and an owner able to continue. Report this as a demonstrated department operating cycle; it is not evidence of paid customer demand.

An operating plan can be ready for an authorized test before an MVC is demonstrated. Use these distinct claims:

| Claim | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Prepared for the next experiment | Bounded offer, named owner, evidence-based experiment, time/cash cap, acceptance and stop criteria, and authority for the next action. Material dependencies are named. |
| MVC demonstrated for this offer and scope | At least one observed customer cycle reaches agreed acceptance and exchange; actual costs/cash are reconciled; a feasible next cycle has an owner, capacity and applicable permissions. State date, scope and limits. |
| Department operating cycle demonstrated for this scope | At least one observed internal delivery reaches agreed user/stakeholder and sponsor acceptance; actual budget and capacity use and outstanding obligations are reconciled; the next cycle has authorized resources, an owner and applicable permissions. State date, scope and limits. |
| Repetition demonstrated | Further observed cycles support the specific repeatability claim under the relevant conditions. One successful cycle is insufficient. |

These are operating claims. Incorporation, a polished pack, a working prototype and passing package tests do not establish them. Neither an MVC nor a department baseline guarantees ARR, repeat demand, profitability, product-market fit, scalable demand, legal clearance or investment readiness. A business or department with a longer delivery, acceptance or payment cycle remains at its observed state; do not redefine the cycle to meet the target. A deliberately free experiment may validate use or delivery, but it does not establish paid demand. Departments and other noncommercial programs use their commissioned outcome and funding evidence under the existing program or initiative route.

## Start with the smallest useful record

Recover existing evidence and decisions first. Use the questions below to define the offer, route to first use, delivery requirements, owner and governing constraints. Identify the hardest unproven assumption and next permitted action.

| Working question | Founder / company | Internal department |
|---|---|---|
| Who needs and accepts the work? | Customer, user and payer; name the acceptance owner. | Internal users and stakeholders; name the delivery recipient, acceptance owner and accountable sponsor. |
| What is offered first? | A bounded product or service, price or test price, outcome and exclusions. | A bounded internal service or product, requested outcome, service scope and exclusions. |
| How does the first cycle begin? | Customer source, concrete sales ask, owner, test metric and applicable outreach authority. | Request/intake route, sponsor mandate, first internal user, owner and adoption or delivery metric. |
| What supports delivery? | Authorized cash, evidenced capacity and the customer's agreed terms. | Approved budget/resource allocation, actually available people/tools and agreed delivery/acceptance terms. |
| Who decides and handles failure? | Accountable founder or decision owner, with reserved decisions and support/refund obligations. | Accountable sponsor and operating owner, with delegated decisions, escalation, rework and cancellation responsibilities. |

Listen to the intended users and collect permitted evidence. Choose the smallest useful offer and set acceptance and stop criteria before testing. Build only the delivery capability needed, perform the authorized cycle, then reconcile its outcome and resources. Save a short next-cycle procedure and name the decision owner so another authorized operator can continue. A document pack alone does not establish this operating baseline.

When direct customer evidence is the next dependency, use the optional [customer-discovery and price-testing guide](customer-discovery.md), selected [call-card prompts](../templates/call-card.md#optional-customer-discovery-prompts) and [commercial experiment fields](../templates/experiment-card.md#optional-commercial-test-details). They make recent behavior, current alternatives, buying authority, exact offers and contrary evidence inspectable within Frame/Test. They add no universal interview quota, new gate or mandatory record; departments use the relevant internal-user and resource questions.

Minimum requirements say what must work for the promised outcome and how failure is detected; a full product specification is optional. Ask only about consequential gaps that cannot be resolved from authorized sources. Use a [company brief](../templates/company-brief.md) if these are scattered; existing sufficient records need no duplicate brief. For a department, explain the internal counterparts in its existing fields: sponsor/resource authority alongside the customer/user/payer field, internal service and acceptance in offer/requirements, and intake/adoption in the customer route. Mark commercial price, sales and collection questions not applicable only with a scoped basis; preserve the template and record names.

Use 00 for locations, selected profile, owners and current work; 03 for the adopted position and decision rights; 09 for material unknowns and the evidence that would settle them; 10 for actual decisions, gate results and revisions. The brief links to those records where they already hold the answer. File 04 or a sufficient existing model holds economics or the department's budget, cost per outcome and capacity. File 05 is created only when a material jurisdiction or activity question needs a durable map. No deck, organizational chart, CRM, background routine or full context tree is mandatory.

Do not present a template as an operating company or department. In a reusable kit, blank inputs are appropriate. In commissioned work, retrieve available inputs, populate required content and identify the exact remaining blockers.

## Evidence gates for the next commitment

Company or department readiness is an evidence axis separate from the playbook's Ingestion, Firming, Mandate, Blitzing and Delivering work stages. The one-or-two-day launch target does not change the gates or replace the separately selected 72-hour measured pilot. Revisit only gates affected by new evidence, scope or conditions. An operating unit can deliver a safe service while a proposed regulated feature remains on Hold.

Before each material experiment or commitment, define observable pass, change and stop criteria, the evidence source, owner, deadline or observation horizon, and maximum cash/time exposure. Choose thresholds for the cost of being wrong. There is no universal interview count, conversion target, revenue target or number of documents.

| Gate | Evidence that supports the next step | Typical Hold condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame** | Named customer/payer or internal user/sponsor and problem; bounded offer with price or authorized resource envelope; measurable outcome and exclusions; accountable owner; hardest assumption; permitted next test and cash/time cap. Distinguish observed problems from owner beliefs. | The next action lacks an owner, authority, a bounded risk or an intelligible recipient outcome. |
| **Test** | Direct evidence from the intended customer/payer or internal user/sponsor, with the behavior observed and its limits. An interview establishes what was said; a booking, contract, payment, internal request and budget approval establish different things. Evaluate against the criteria fixed before the test. | Praise or budget approval is being treated as demand or adoption; the buying/resource decision or critical requirement is untested; exposure exceeds the authorized cap. |
| **Deliver** | Agreed scope and acceptance, actual delivery evidence, customer acceptance or department user/stakeholder and sponsor acceptance (or the exact rejection), applicable support/refund/rework path, and a named owner for failure. Use real artifacts and receipts for live claims. | Delivery is unsafe, the promised result cannot be checked, required permissions are unresolved, or fulfillment/cancellation responsibilities are unclear. |
| **Operate** | Reconciled exchange and obligations, contribution and cash timing for a company; reconciled actual resource use, budget and obligations for a department. Both need realistic capacity and dependencies, plus a short next-cycle procedure with owner, checks, failure route and stop conditions. State what one cycle does and does not prove. | Collection or resource availability is assumed, unpaid obligations are omitted, cash falls below the decision owner's floor or budget is exceeded, labor/capacity is missing, or repeating delivery depends on an unavailable person or permission. |

Record Proceed, Hold or Walk for the specific next commitment, with its evidence and permitted continuing work. Proceed requires the applicable evidence and existing authority; a gate does not grant approval. Hold pauses only the dependent commitment while safe preparation continues. Walk rejects that option for the stated scope, preserving useful evidence. A decision owner can revise a commercial preference or risk budget within their authority; an override cannot waive law, another person's rights, required permissions or host restrictions.

Failed tests should change the offer, method or decision. Keep the original criteria and observed result, then record any amended test separately. Never improve a result by rewriting its threshold afterward.

## Match rigor to consequences

| Next action | Proportionate evidence and control |
|---|---|
| Reversible internal exploration using permitted nonsensitive material | A bounded assumption and small time/cash budget may suffice. Label the output and avoid unsupported customer claims. |
| Customer or internal delivery, spending, sensitive information or consequential claims | Verify load-bearing inputs, customer or user/sponsor acceptance, commercial terms or resource authority, data handling, recovery/refund/rework obligations and the human authority for the exact commitment. Use independent review when a material defect would harm the recipient or operating unit. |
| Activity involving regulated services, customer funds, health/safety, material security exposure or difficult-to-reverse obligations | Identify activity, jurisdictions and parties. Obtain current primary-source evidence and qualified legal, tax, licensing, security or domain review where needed. Resolve material scope questions before the affected commitment. A mockup or sandbox test can proceed only within its own permitted scope. |

Industry labels do not decide the gate. The same company can have low-risk internal work and a high-consequence customer action. Neither a generic disclaimer nor a founder's or sponsor's assertion resolves a substantive constraint.

## People and operating authority

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Accountable founder, department sponsor or decision owner | Owns the offer or mandate, risk/resource budget and decisions; specifies the operating owner, delegation and reserved decisions; accepts or changes the position of record. |
| Operator or builder | Produces and checks the authorized work, preserves evidence, and escalates a material exception with a concrete recommendation. An AI assistant can fill this role within granted authority. |
| Reviewer or qualified adviser | Independently checks the consequential artifact or scoped domain question when required. A reviewed artifact is not an authorization to execute it. |
| Canonical scribe | Maintains the one current position and necessary receipts; reconciles revision-bound DELTAs and interrupted actions. |

One human may fill several roles in a small company or department. Add people only for capacity, expertise or genuine independence. AI does not become a legal officer, account holder, signer, employer or licensed professional through a role label.

Use explicit task authority for outreach, publication, formation/filing, contracts, payments, account or credential changes, hiring and production deployment. Existing specific authorization remains valid within its recorded scope and limits; do not ask again merely because a gate has advanced. A broad request to prepare a company or department is not authority to perform these actions. Prepare the concrete, reviewable result up to the boundary, then obtain only the missing decision. Retain actual action receipts and reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying.

## Cash, economics and capacity

Before money is committed, show a rough model covering the next material cash obligations and the decision horizon. Use [company-cash.csv](../templates/company-cash.csv), file 04 or an existing sufficient model. Choose explicit periods, currency, as-of date, units and sources. Separate actuals, estimates and assumptions. Unknown is not zero. A cash model with incomplete inputs is incomplete even when a subtotal can be computed.

For a department, use the existing [04 Economics And Budget][department-budget] program budget fields or a sufficient authorized internal model. Record allocation and approval scope, committed/paid/cancellable amounts, actual resource use, cost per accepted outcome where supported, available delivery hours and the next-cycle funding/resource dependency. An allocation, notional saving or cost avoidance is not a cash receipt, earned revenue or evidence of a sale. Do not put internal budgets into commercial CSV fields to make the calculator fit; use the existing records when its cash/revenue schema does not represent the department's economics. The helper's contribution output is commercial contribution, not department value or budget efficiency. A department with actual external sales must keep that commercial evidence distinct.

For a commercial model, distinguish booked orders, earned revenue, invoices, gross collections and refunds; variable incurred delivery costs from cash payments; fixed and one-time costs; tax payment timing; financing from revenue; unrestricted opening cash from customer or restricted funds. Track committed but unpaid obligations in the appropriate future periods. Never count customer funds held for others as working capital.

Compute and inspect the applicable financial and capacity measures in code:

* Closing cash = opening cash + gross cash receipts + financing in - refunds paid - variable cash costs - fixed cash costs - one-time cash costs - financing out - tax paid.
* Contribution = earned revenue, net of revenue reductions, minus variable incurred delivery costs. State what those costs include. Contribution per unit and margin require valid nonzero denominators and consistent units/periods.
* Available cash above the chosen floor = closing cash minus the reserve floor. A negative value identifies a funding/timing problem for a decision; it does not authorize borrowing or spending. A reserve floor is a planning control, not a cash outflow.
* Capacity = actual or evidenced delivery hours required versus available delivery hours in the same period. Include founder or department staff work, support, rework and bottlenecks. Unpaid founder time is still a capacity cost; where decision-useful, show its imputed economic cost separately with a stated rate.

Avoid double counting a refund or paid founder compensation across categories. Revenue and costs may belong to a different period from their receipts/payments. A constant-burn runway ratio is only a rough diagnostic when its assumptions hold; dated cash obligations and collections govern the next commitment. Stress the decisive assumptions, such as slower collection, lower sales, higher delivery cost or founder unavailability. State the action that would follow a shortfall.

The optional `setup/company.py` helper creates local starter records and computes the declared CSV schedule. Its initializer emits company-labelled drafts with Type set to venture; it has no department mode. To reuse that draft for a department, set Type to program or initiative as appropriate in file 00, record the sponsor and operating owner, and explain the internal counterparts in the brief and open questions before adoption. Keep the paths, command names and CSV fields unchanged. Alternatively, start directly from sufficient existing records. Follow its `--help` for supported commands and validation. It cannot infer missing costs, verify sources, resolve tax treatment, approve an action or certify company or department readiness. No helper is required to use this profile.

For ventures and departments, the optional [delivery-capacity command](../setup/delivery-capacity.md) assesses a separate supplied resource plan in hours. It identifies shared-resource overloads, uncovered effort, missed due periods and work scheduled before its dependencies finish. Supply availability net of other obligations and retain unknowns as null; do not treat budgets as available staff hours. The assessment does not allocate people, optimize a schedule or adopt a commitment. Preserve its input hash and reconcile conflicts with the owner before using the plan for the next cycle.

## Jurisdiction and activity checks, only where needed

For the proposed next activity, identify the relevant location of operation, customer, worker, entity and money/data flow. Consider formation/registration, tax and invoicing, contracts, employment/contractors, privacy/security, intellectual property, sector licenses and insurance only to the extent they can affect that activity. These are discovery prompts, not a complete legal checklist and not a claim that every company requires each item.

Reuse existing verified advice or obtain current primary sources and qualified advice where the consequence warrants it. Record the issue, scope, source/date, owner, unresolved question, next commitment affected, and the event that requires refresh. A finding of not applicable needs a scoped basis; unknown stays unknown. Neither an unregulated industry label nor incorporation proves compliance. Filing, signing, opening accounts and paying charges remain separately authorized actions. The kit performs none automatically.

## Acceptance and continuation

Deliver the commissioned artifact and an evidence-bound readiness statement: what has been demonstrated, for which offer/customer or department/user scope and date, the next permitted action, and any material blocker. Use the existing [acceptance record](../templates/acceptance-template.md) when a durable record is useful. Keep accepted decisions separate from factual verification.

For a company or department pack, another authorized operator should be able to identify the offer or mandate, current evidence, obligations, accountable owner, next cycle and stop conditions using the saved records. Test that handoff proportionately. Export only the requested allowlisted content under the existing [handoff contract](../templates/handoff.md); customer records, credentials and private material are not reusable kit content.

In the master kit, the [synthetic service example](../examples/company/service-example.md) shows the whole reasoning path. The [synthetic regulated hold](../examples/company/regulated-hold.md) shows why a prototype and money model may be complete while a live commitment remains blocked. Examples are training fixtures, deliberately excluded from runtime views, and are not required to execute this profile. They provide no real demand, transaction, legal or performance evidence.
