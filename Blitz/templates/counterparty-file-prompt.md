# Counterparty file prompt

the owner sends this to the counterparty, for their own AI assistant or their team. It asks them to package what they have, with a manifest, so ingestion starts from complete, dated sources.

```text
Please gather every document you hold on [PROJECT] and send them as one folder or zip. Include a manifest with one line per file:

file name | title | author | date written | date last changed | version | who it was made for | pages or length | anything missing or unreadable

Please include what applies: decks, business or program plans, financial models or budgets, licence, mandate and registration evidence, bank, capital or budget approval evidence, agreements and term sheets, product or program specifications, screenshots or mockups, and recordings or transcripts of past meetings.

Mark anything confidential. Do not summarize the documents; send the originals.
```
