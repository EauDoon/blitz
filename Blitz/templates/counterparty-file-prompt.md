# Counterparty file prompt

The owner fills the brackets before sending. Keep the scope and handling terms inside the copied request. A confidentiality label does not establish permission to disclose.

```text
For [PROJECT], we need evidence to answer [DECISION / PURPOSE]. Please provide only [DOCUMENT CATEGORIES / SPECIFIC QUESTIONS] covering [DATE RANGE]. If the scope, recipient or handling terms below are unclear, hold the affected material and ask us to resolve them.

Share only material you are authorized to disclose to [NAMED RECIPIENTS / ACCESS GROUP], including any required third-party permission. Exclude credentials, unrelated personal information, private advice and material restricted from these recipients. A relevant document is not automatically authorized for sharing.

Use the minimum necessary material: a relevant excerpt or redacted copy is preferable when it answers the question. Retain dates, units and context needed to interpret it; identify redactions, missing pages and limitations without revealing the withheld content. Send originals only when necessary for this purpose and permitted. For recordings or transcripts, confirm that the applicable permissions cover this reuse and these recipients; otherwise provide authorized notes or mark the evidence unavailable.

Transfer through [APPROVED ROUTE], with access limited to [ACCESS GROUP]. Do not use an unapproved public link or upload material to an AI service unless that processing is authorized. [OWNER] is responsible for access and deletion/return at [DATE OR RETENTION TRIGGER]. Confirm these terms before transfer.

Include a manifest:
file name | title | author/source | date written | date changed | version | intended audience | excerpt/redaction status | disclosure authority/limitations | anything missing or unreadable

If evidence is unavailable or cannot be shared, say which question remains unanswered. Do not replace it with an unsupported summary or invented evidence.
```
