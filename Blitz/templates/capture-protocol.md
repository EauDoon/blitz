# Capture protocol

For a commissioned meeting capture or required pilot record, capture the following. For narrow later continuation, use [Operating Loop](../skills/blitz/references/operating-loop.md) and the relevant existing evidence; do not generate a full retrospective debrief merely because notes exist:

* Start time and end time, DD-MM-YYYY h:mm AM/PM with the time zone.
* Attendees, with their roles.
* Where the recording, transcript, photos and notes are stored.
* Speaker labels, checked the same day by someone who was there.
* Any commitment said aloud, word for word where possible.

When a full debrief is required, it comes from the transcript, not from memory, within 1 hour. A debrief from memory in the first pilot missed three points the transcript held.
