A venture fills the applicable cost, revenue and cash view. A program fills budget and cost per outcome for its KPI. Every consequential figure shows source or labelled assumption, currency, units, period and arithmetic run in code. Rough model first; expose unaffordable scope or unsupported economics before polish. A model does not establish funding, actual demand, permission to spend or company readiness.

Use a sufficient existing model rather than duplicate it. For a company, `templates/company-cash.csv` is an optional input format and `profiles/company.md` defines the evidence gates. Blank/unknown is not zero. Keep actuals, estimates and assumptions distinct; reconcile the narrative to the model revision.

Keep interest, contracted orders, earned revenue, invoices, collections and refunds separate. State net versus gross treatment, delivery/recognition basis and collection timing. A signed order or raised invoice is not cash. Exclude financing from revenue and customer/restricted money from usable working capital.

## Cash timing, contribution and capacity

Use the next material obligation horizon, explicit periods and one currency per consistent schedule; handle any conversion separately with dated rates and evidence. Record unrestricted opening cash, collections, refunds, variable/fixed/one-time payments, tax timing, financing in/out, closing cash and the decision owner's reserve floor. Include committed unpaid obligations when they fall due. The floor is a constraint, not another outflow. Sources/status and period definitions belong here or in the linked company brief.

Compute closing cash from opening cash plus receipts/financing in minus the distinct outflow categories. Carry a closing balance into the next opening balance; investigate any break. A cash shortfall or missing material input holds the affected commitment, not independent preparation.

Contribution is earned revenue net of reductions minus variable incurred delivery costs. State included costs. Per-unit contribution, margins and break-even assumptions require consistent periods/units and valid denominators. Show unpaid founder time and its capacity requirement; use an explicitly labelled imputed rate only when decision-useful, without treating it as a cash payment. Compare all delivery/support/rework hours with actually available hours and identify the bottleneck.
