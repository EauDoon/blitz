# Synthetic company example: customer-funds feature on Hold

**SYNTHETIC FIXTURE ONLY.** All names, statements, evidence and outcomes below are fictional. No jurisdiction has been researched, no regulated activity performed, and no legal or licensing conclusion is supplied. The example tests the boundary between useful preparation and authority to operate.

## Proposed activity and available evidence

`Sample Payment Concept` proposes collecting customer money, holding it overnight and forwarding it to merchants. `Sample Founder` is the accountable human owner. The intended operator, contracting entity, locations of customers/merchants and actual money flow are not yet settled.

The source set contains only three supplied fixture excerpts:

* S1, founder statement: "Our partner is licensed, so we should be covered." This is an unverified claim. No license, jurisdiction, permitted activity, agency agreement or legal analysis is supplied.
* S2, prototype result: a local simulation routes synthetic ledger entries between fictional parties, with no bank, payment network, real funds, credentials or personal data. The test checks its specified arithmetic only.
* S3, task authorization: prepare a local options memo and synthetic money-flow diagram. Do not contact partners/customers, accept funds, open accounts, sign, file, deploy or spend.

There are no actual customer commitments, payment receipts or delivery evidence. A working simulation is not a paid commercial cycle.

## Gate decision and useful deliverable

**Hold the live customer-funds activity.** The dependency is material: the proposed activity, responsible parties, locations, permission/contract scope and money handling are unresolved. A partner's reported license cannot establish that this entity and proposed arrangement may operate. Do not declare the model compliant or prohibited from the available evidence.

The authorized options memo can be complete with this decision table:

| Option | What can be evaluated now | Evidence before a live commitment |
|---|---|---|
| The company receives and forwards money | Synthetic flow, roles, failure points and rough cost assumptions | Exact activity/entity/jurisdiction; current relevant primary sources; actual license/permission coverage where applicable; contractual allocation and qualified review; safeguarding, reconciliation, loss/refund and operational responsibilities |
| An authorized provider controls the entire money flow | A proposal showing the company excluded from possession/control of funds | Evidence that the actual flow and contracts support that arrangement; applicable provider and company obligations; current scoped qualified review where needed. Label this a proposal, not an approved workaround. |
| A reporting-only prototype | Local simulation using fictional ledger entries and no real integrations | Existing S3 authority suffices for this internal artifact. Any later real data, external access, offer or customer delivery needs its own scoped evidence and authority. |

The memo's requested artifact acceptance can pass if it accurately describes the options, sources and unresolved issues. Company readiness remains unproved, and the live feature remains on Hold. A cash model cannot release this gate.
