## Program pack outputs

* **Charter one pager.** Objective: 40 funded teams that ship to mainnet in 6 months. Out of scope: equity, token allocations, marketing grants.
* **Budget and cost per outcome.** USD 1,200,000 in total: USD 1,000,000 of grants and USD 200,000 of operations. Cost per shipped team at 40 teams: 1,200,000 / 40 = USD 30,000. At the bear case of 25 teams: 1,200,000 / 25 = USD 48,000. Arithmetic run in code.
* **Stakeholder and partner map.** Committee, three partner auditors, two hackathon hosts. One partner can block payouts: the auditor that signs milestone reviews.
* **KPI tree.** Shipped teams, broken down into applications, then accepted teams, then milestone 1 passes, then mainnet launches.
* **Experiment card.** Assumption: 150 qualified applications arrive in 3 weeks from partner channels alone. Cheapest test: open the form to two partner channels for 7 days. Threshold: 50 qualified applications. Decision it changes: whether to buy paid outreach.

## What differed from a venture run

* Hold was triggered by a budget approval, not by money raised or a licence.
* No regulatory map. The veto role in the skill map stayed empty, and cold-read covered outbound copy.
* The hardest assumption was adoption (applications), not a commercial one.
* The clock, the gates, the scribe rule and the document pipeline ran unchanged.
