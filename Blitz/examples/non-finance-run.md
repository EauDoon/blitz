# Example run: Folio Box (synthetic, non-finance)

Folio Box is invented: an independent bookshop group testing a monthly book subscription. All names, figures and times are sample data. The example shows that the stages and gates carry over to a venture outside finance.

| Stage | What happened | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Ingestion (T+0h to T+6h) | 7 sources: a customer survey, supplier price lists, two past campaign reports, the owners' brief. The survey's sample size was not stated; recorded as a gap | Proceed: no unresolved assumption could invalidate the next commitment |
| Firming (T+6h to T+20h) | One call with the two owners. Position of record v1: a paid pilot for 300 subscribers in one city, three price points tested | Owners confirmed scope, success measure (60% renew after month 2), budget cap and decision rights |
| Mandate (T+20h to T+44h) | One pager, a rough unit model (box cost, postage, margin per price point, run in code), GTM hypotheses, and one experiment card: a waitlist page with a price shown, target 150 sign-ups in 10 days | Each output met its acceptance criteria |
| Blitzing (T+36h to T+56h) | Instructions refreshed; the landing page went through frontend-taste; no project skill built | Context integrity passed |
| Delivering (T+56h to T+66h) | A supplier brief ran end to end. Recovery drill RC03: a new postage price list made the model and the one pager stale; both were refreshed within 40 min | Rehearsal and drill recorded |

Skills that fired, from receipts: meeting-to-action, doc-style, cold-read, calc-workbook, reasoning-core (sign-up forecast as a scenario table), frontend-taste, frontend-scroll-craft.

What changed compared with a finance venture: no regulatory map was needed (file 05 never created), and the Hold rule never applied. The clock, the scribe rule and the gates worked unchanged.
