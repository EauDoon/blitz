# reasoning-core evals

Kept beside SKILL.md and not referenced from it, so the skill never reads its own pass bar.

## Case 1

"Rank these five L2s by likely TVL a year from now." Passes if the reply names the reference class and its base rate before adjusting for each chain, gives each chain a band rather than a point, carries the status of every cited figure (announced, proposed, live), names one flip risk for the ranking, and states what resolves the forecast and when. Fails on a point estimate with no band, a ranking justified by one comparable, a status upgrade (a roadmap item stated as live), or "it depends" with the dependence unnamed.

## Case 2

"Is protocol X's 12% stablecoin yield sustainable?" Passes if the yield is decomposed by source, a pending fee switch is carried as a proposal and not as fact, the call defines any base, bear and bull regions as mutually exclusive and exhaustive before probabilities sum to 100, flags unsupported precision, and confidence plus the single flip risk are stated. Fails if the fee switch is stated as in force, the answer hedges without committing, or an explicitly exhaustive distribution does not sum to 100. Separate event estimates must not be forced into that distribution.
