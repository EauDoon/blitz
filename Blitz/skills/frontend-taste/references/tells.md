# The tells, banned outright

Merged from impeccable.style/slop and taste-skill section 9. None of these ship unless the brief explicitly asks for one.

**Surface and decoration**

* Thick colored accent border on a rounded card, the most recognizable tell in the set
* Neon and outer glow; radial gradient halos and spotlight glows on dark
* Decorative grid lines and crosshairs that organize nothing
* Hairline border plus a wide diffuse shadow on the same element
* Border radius at or above 24px, the soft blob
* Repeating gradient stripe texture as surface decoration
* Pure black `#000000`; use off black or zinc-950
* Gradient text on headings and metrics
* Custom mouse cursors
* Glassmorphism used decoratively rather than functionally

**Structure and labels**

* Section number eyebrows: `01`, `001 Capabilities`, `06 how it works`, `00 / INDEX`
* `01 / 4` pagination on images and bento tiles
* Version labels in the hero: `V0.6`, `BETA`, `EARLY ACCESS`, unless the brief is a launch
* Version footers on marketing pages: `v1.4.2`, `Build 0048`, `last sync 4s ago`
* Generic step labels: `Stage 1`, `Step 1`, `Phase 01`. The verb is the label.
* Icon tile in a rounded square stacked above a heading
* Three identical feature cards in a row
* Cards nested inside cards inside cards
* Hero metric strip: three big numbers, small labels, gradient accents
* The middle dot rationed to one per line, never as the default separator
* Decoration text strip at the hero bottom: `BRAND. MOTION. SPATIAL.`
* Vertical rotated text
* Pills and tags overlaid on images; decorative photo credit captions
* Locale, city, time or weather strips unless the brand is genuinely place based
* Scroll cues of any kind: `Scroll`, arrow, animated mouse wheel
* `border-t` and `border-b` on every row of a long list
* Filled background track progress bars as a comparison visual on a marketing page
* Micro meta sentences under an eyebrow

**Motion**

* Pulsing status dots on static data; decorative colored dots before nav items, list rows and badges
* Blinking cursors on non editable copy
* Auto scrolling marquees demanding attention
* Bounce and elastic easing on interface elements
* Image scale or rotate on hover

**Production defects that read as slop**

* Uncaught script errors; content invisible at rest because a reveal handler never fired
* Text lines past 80 characters; body text touching the viewport edge with no container padding
* Content overflowing its container or forcing a horizontal scrollbar
* Tooltips and menus clipped by an overflow container
* Padding under 8px, target 12 to 16px in containers
* Justified text without hyphenation
* Skipped heading levels
* One column stretching far past its neighbors with dead space
