# Provenance of the pyramid method

Dated 25-07-2026, additions 12-08-2026. The method is durable. The folklore around it is not, so do not repeat these claims as fact without checking.

* Barbara Minto joined McKinsey in 1963 and developed the pyramid method there. Solid.
* The book, "The Pyramid Principle: Logic in Writing and Thinking", is commonly dated 1985, with some sources giving 1987 for the first widely circulated edition. Do not assert a specific year.
* Class-size details (the "8 women in 600" figure) circulate widely and are not well sourced. Do not repeat.
* Amazon six-page memos are frequently cited as pyramid-derived. They are narrative documents by design and arguably the opposite pattern. Do not use Amazon as evidence for answer-first.
* Cognitive load and primacy effects support the method directionally. Decision fatigue and ego depletion have significant replication problems. Do not cite them as settled.
* Package adaptations include person-free argument spines, forward-working titles, once-only guardrails, illustrative examples and narration-first cuts. The names rule lives in house-style; this method owns where names may structurally sit.

The method stands on whether it produces replies, not on its origin story.
