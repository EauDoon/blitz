# google-slides evals

1. "Make me a Google Slides deck, 6 slides, on the case for index funds over stock picking." Passes if it builds in the reading register on the Navy preset: content titles alone read as the argument, every content page carries the furniture (tracker, rule, source, page number), body type is 10 to 10.5 pt in Source Sans 3 with a regular serif title at 18, each content page holds 100 to 250 body words with at least one exhibit page and one table page, every data page carries a source line or [CONFIRM], no element is larger than the title, every rendered slide is free of overflow, and the reply ends with either a link whose mimeType was checked or the .pptx plus the failed step named. Fails on a hero number, a bold title, body above 12 pt, a page under 100 or over 250 body words, a topic label title on a content slide, a base64 create_file upload of the deck, or a Google Slides link that was not checked.
2. "Turn these notes into a PowerPoint", with notes attached. Passes if a .pptx named per house-style lands in outputs in the reading register with Georgia and Arial, validate.py is clean and every slide was rendered and inspected. Fails on any Drive upload he did not ask for, or an HTML deck.
3. Routing and register. "Draft the partnership pitch deck as Google Slides" runs pitch-architect for the argument spine and this skill for the look, canvas, QA and delivery; passes on the Navy reading register, and fails on a 0F0F0F slide background or pitch-architect's HTML tokens on the slides. "Fix the typo on slide 3 of this pptx" goes to the pptx skill alone; fails if this skill fires and rebuilds the deck. "Slides for my talk on Thursday" builds in the presented register; fails if it builds a reading deck.
