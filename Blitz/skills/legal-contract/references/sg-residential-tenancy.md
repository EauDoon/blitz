# Singapore private residential tenancy: rules and to-dos

Verified 26-09-2026 unless marked (unverified). Re-verify anything with a rate, a date or a penalty before quoting it. Run this file on any Singapore residential tenancy agreement, on either side.

## Before signing

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Term | Minimum stay three consecutive months for private residential property; no daily or weekly letting | URA, ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/renting-property |
| Occupancy | Cap of 6 unrelated persons; units of 90 sqm or more may register (S$20) for up to 8 from 22-01-2024 to 31-12-2028 (re-read on ura.gov.sg 27-09-2026); domestic helpers excluded; owners are liable | URA, same page |
| Short-term accommodation | Under three consecutive months is banned in all private residential types; fine up to S$5,000, higher court fines for repeat cases | URA, ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/short-term-accommodation |
| Immigration checks | Landlord must sight original passport and immigration or work pass (physical or digital), confirm the tenant owns them, check every occupier, keep records 5 years, re-check on a change of occupier; harbouring is an offence under Immigration Act s 57(1)(d), negligent harbouring up to S$6,000 fine or 12 months jail | ICA anti-harbouring page; CEA practice guidelines effective 24-03-2023 |
| Pass check | ICA passes at go.gov.sg/check-ica-pass; work passes at go.gov.sg/check-work-pass (MOM e-service, landlord or landlord's agent may use it; needs date of birth plus FIN or passport number) or the SGWorkPass app; CEA landlord checklist wants pass validity of at least 3 months | ICA, MOM, CEA checklist v1.1 |
| Agent forms | CEA prescribed Form 3 (non-exclusive) or Form 7 (exclusive, lapses at the stated date or 3 months, whichever earlier) for a landlord, Forms 4 and 8 for a tenant (titles re-read on cea.gov.sg 27-09-2026); an agent may not collect commission from both sides; pay the agency, not the salesperson; no fixed rates | CEA, cea.gov.sg/consumers/engaging-a-property-agent/renting-or-renting-out |
| Commission convention | Landlord's agent: about half a month for a 1 year lease, one month for 2 years (market practice, unverified); Form 7 cl 6: landlord not liable to pay a co-broking agent | Form 7; market guides |
| Template | CEA "Private Residential Tenancy Agreement" v1.4 (12-08-2026), not compulsory. Its break clause: exercisable after [ ] months, [ ] months' notice or rent in lieu, with documentary evidence of the transfer or cessation of employment. Item 12 names who pays stamp fees. Landlord warrants the financial institution's prior consent where mortgaged, and covenants to insure against fire and pay outgoings | CEA template PDF on isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg |
| Mortgage | Where the unit is mortgaged, the bank's consent or notice is normally required by the mortgage instrument (typical wording "not to lease or part with possession without the Bank's prior written consent", unverified per bank); the CEA template makes the landlord warrant it | CEA template; OCBC mortgage T&C |
| Insurance | Mortgage terms require the property to be insured; the CEA template has the landlord insure against fire. Telling the insurer the unit is let is a material fact to disclose (unverified per insurer) | OCBC T&C; CEA template |
| Deposit | Convention one month per year of lease; state that it cannot be set off against the last months' rent | Market practice |
| Diplomatic clause | Convention: exercisable after 12 months, 2 months' notice, documentary proof, partial commission refund by the tenant; narrow the triggers to transfer out of Singapore, cessation of employment in Singapore with departure, or refused entry | Market practice; CEA template item 14 |
| E-signature | The Electronic Transactions Act First Schedule excludes "any contract for the sale or other disposition of immovable property, or any interest in such property". Assessment, not settled law: a lease is an interest in land, so a DocuSign tenancy likely sits outside the ETA safe harbour and rests on the common law functional test, which Singapore courts have applied to typed names in email. Keep the DocuSign certificate; offer wet-ink counterparts if either side asks | ETA 2010 First Schedule; CMS e-signature guide |
| Disputes | Small Claims Tribunal: up to S$20,000 (S$30,000 with a memorandum of consent), residential tenancy of not more than 2 years, file within 2 years, respondent in Singapore | judiciary.gov.sg/civil/cases-eligible-small-claim |

## After signing, with deadlines

| To-do | Deadline | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stamp duty | 14 days after signing in Singapore; 30 days after receipt in Singapore if signed overseas | 0.4% of total rent for the term (lease of 4 years or less), on the higher of contractual or market rent; the tenant is liable by law unless the lease says otherwise, and pays by convention; e-Stamp at IRAS and check the certificate under Stamp Duty, Check Stamp Cert Authenticity | Stamp Duties Act Third Schedule Art 8; IRAS Taxbytes; ask.gov.sg/iras |
| Late stamping | Immediately if missed | Penalty up to 4 times the duty; fees cannot be waived; an unstamped lease is inadmissible in civil proceedings until duty and penalty are paid (SDA s 52) | ask.gov.sg/iras; 2014 SGHC 62; 2017 SGCA 47 |
| IRAS, property tax | Before the tenant moves in | A let unit loses the owner-occupier concession; inform IRAS; non-owner-occupier rates from 01-01-2024: 12% on the first S$30,000 of annual value, 20% on the next S$15,000, 28% on the next S$15,000, 36% above S$60,000; tax due 31 January | gov.sg explainer; MOF newsroom; ask.gov.sg/iras |
| IRAS, income tax | In the year's return | Rental income is taxable; either 15% deemed expenses plus mortgage interest, or actual expenses (property tax, maintenance, minor repairs, fire insurance, interest) | IRAS e-Tax guide 3rd ed 30-01-2026 |
| Bank | Before handover | Give the consent or notice the facility letter requires | Facility letter (verify the file's own wording) |
| Insurer | Before handover | Tell the insurer the unit is let | Policy terms (unverified) |
| Records | 5 years | Keep pass checks, the signed TA, the stamp certificate, the inventory and the DocuSign certificate | CEA practice guidelines |
